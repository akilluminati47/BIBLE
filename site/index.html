Where the numbers come from

      
The splash is drawn from BIBLE's own layout, font, logo and button sheet, and the sky and towers from RIPPS2's. The towers stop at 3.2 s, where RIPPS2's menu comes in and a legacy theme takes over.

      
Black time is measured from PCSX2 frames with RIPPS2 on a memory card. The console figure is an estimate: only RIPPS2's own start was timed on a real SCPH-39001, from its DIAG.TXT. The drive wait is the lab's too.

      
Both TVs are fan-made in code from published sizes and photographs. Open the model studio.
